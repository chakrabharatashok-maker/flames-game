📖 How Does FLAMES Actually Work? (The Classic Rules) ▾
1

Write Names

Take the first name of both partners (ignoring spaces and special characters).

2

Cross Common Letters

For every letter that appears in both names, strike off matching pairs.

3

Count Leftovers (N)

Count the total number of uncrossed letters remaining across both names.

4

FLAMES Elimination

Count up to N in cyclic order on F-L-A-M-E-S. Eliminate the N-th letter and start the next count from the next active letter until one remains!

FLAMES Meaning:
F – Friends L – Lovers A – Affectionate M – Marriage E – Enemies S – Siblings
